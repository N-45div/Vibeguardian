How it works
Real Google, real keys, nothing simulated

Delete a key. Watch Google judge it.

Press the button and VibeGuardian makes a throwaway Gemini key, gets Gemini 4 Argon to answer with it, deletes it, then keeps asking from five places until every place refuses it 12 rounds in a row. The key never leaves the server; you see its fingerprint.
